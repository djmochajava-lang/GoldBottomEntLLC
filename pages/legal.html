Ticket Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy applies to tickets sold by Gold Bottom Ent LLC for L.A. Young & Soul Society performances. It tells you when a ticket is refundable, when it is not, and what you can do with a ticket you already own. It is here so you can read it before you buy, not after.

1. Refunds We Issue Automatically

You do not need to ask and you do not need to do anything. We refund your order in full when:

  • The event is cancelled.
  • The event is postponed by 60 days or more and no new date has been announced.

2. Refunds You Can Request

Ask us and we will refund your order in full when:

  • The show leaves the Washington, DC market. If the event moves to another city, you can have your money back.
  • L.A. Young is not performing. A change to an opening or supporting act does not qualify — the headliner is what this covers.
  • The event is rescheduled to a new date. You have 30 days from the day the new date is announced to ask for a refund.

3. What Is Not Refundable

  • A change of venue. The venue is confirmed and locked in before tickets go on sale, so the venue you saw when you bought is the venue you bought.
  • A change of mind, a schedule conflict, or a personal circumstance.
  • A ticket that has already been scanned. Once your ticket is scanned at the door you have been admitted and the ticket has done its job.

4. Duplicate and Unauthorized Charges

Separate from everything above: if you were charged twice, or a charge was made on your card without your authorization, we reverse it. Tell us and we will fix it.

5. How Refunds Are Paid

  • You get 100% of what you paid. Gold Bottom Ent LLC absorbs the card processing fee — it is not taken out of your refund.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method used for the purchase.
  • Refunds are issued for the whole order. We do not refund part of an order.

6. Giving Your Ticket to Someone Else

  • You may transfer your ticket. Forward the ticket email or the QR code to whoever is going in your place. There is no name change to file and no approval to wait for.
  • Each ticket admits once. The first scan of a QR code gets in; a second scan of the same code is refused at the door. So only pass a ticket to someone you trust to be the one who uses it, and do not plan to use it yourself after you have sent it on.
  • Passing a ticket on does not pass the refund on with it — a refund still goes back to the original payment method (see section 5).

7. How to Ask for a Refund

Email with the confirmation code from your ticket email and the name the order was placed under, and tell us which reason above applies. If we cannot approve a request, we will tell you the specific reason — you will never get a bare "denied."

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any change is posted on this page with a new date.
