About the Artist

L.A. Young is a powerhouse soul, jazz, and blues vocalist with over two decades of live performance — from Blues Alley and City Winery to galas, festivals, and corporate stages. Her performances blend raw emotion with timeless artistry, creating unforgettable experiences for any event.

From intimate jazz club settings to large outdoor festivals, L.A. Young delivers a professional, polished show tailored to your audience and venue requirements.
