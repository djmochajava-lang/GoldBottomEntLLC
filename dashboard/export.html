About Evidence Exports

  • Intake Queue records cover all form submissions and emails processed through the pipeline.
  • Bookings include all booking pipeline records (leads, confirmed, completed).
  • Activity Log captures all create/update/delete operations in the system.
  • By default, PII (names, emails, phone, messages) is redacted for privacy. Enable "Include PII" for full records.
  • Each export includes a SHA-256 checksum for integrity verification.
  • JSON exports contain structured data. CSV exports flatten intake records into spreadsheet-compatible rows.
